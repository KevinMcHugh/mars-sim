Mood Space — charge × grip × valence

Tuning sandbox for docs/mood-space.md. The three axes, the attractor table, and the impact-weighted push/pull blend all ship — their numbers here are seeded from internal/sim/affect.go, so tuning them here is tuning the real thing. Wear ships too, so the fresh/worn pairs here are the real ones. Tag-based trait rules are what is still proposed, and the reason this page exists. Known simplifications: wear here counts each firing, where the sim counts remembered occasions (a run of digs is one), and conversation intensity is a slider rather than conversationMoodVector's formula — the sim exempts conversations from wear entirely.

A scratchpad for mars-sim's proposed mood. Charge (vertical) is how much energy is behind the next action. Grip (horizontal) is how much purchase the colonist feels they have on their situation. Valence (the gauge under the plane) is now stored per colonist and moves only through events, not read live off needs/HP — the same state reads as elated or furious depending on it. Drag the colonist, the home point, or any attractor. Fire events from the table on the right to see them compose.

Charge/grip decay in polar — magnitude fast, angle slow, toward the home point — so intensity reads as mood and direction as temperament. Valence decays on its own, plain linear, slower still (hours-days vs. grip's minutes-hours). Note: the shipped decayAffect is simpler than this — plain per-axis linear decay toward (0,0), no polar split. This page keeps exploring the richer idea; reconciling the two is separable from the rest of this doc.

Attractors — names as a table, not sectors shipped as-is

Identical to affect.go's moodAttractors — same nine points, same radii. Nearest attractor by distance ÷ radius wins; unclaimed space reads as plain "settling".

Trait rules — tags, not a per-kind switch

Each multiplier defaults to ×1 (0 or blank means "no change", matching this codebase's scale-field convention) — a reflection is just ×-1. A rule with no tags matches every event, which is how Resilient/Cowardly (both proposed — not yet Traits) bend wear generally rather than reacting to one kind. Matching is: any of any-tags (or no any-tags at all), none of not-tags.
